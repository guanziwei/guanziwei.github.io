RECENT RESEARCH · 2025–2026

01 / Reinforcement learning

Reinforcement Learning from Ecosystem Feedback

RLEF

I develop RL post-training methods that use ecosystem signals and frozen downstream models to provide rewards for language models. I have deployed these methods in production recommendation systems, connecting model training to outcomes in real use.

My work · Method design, model post-training, evaluation, and production deployment.

Reward designProduction deployment

02 / Language model adaptation

Continuous Pretraining and Post-training

Learning with text and Semantic ID tokens

Starting from pretrained language models, I expand their vocabularies with Semantic ID tokens and train these representations jointly with text. I study data mixtures, model scale, and post-training methods, including SFT and on-policy distillation (OPD), to learn new representations while retaining language capabilities.

My work · Vocabulary expansion, training recipes, scaling experiments, and evaluation across language and Semantic ID tasks.

0.6B to 200B modelsSFT · OPD · RL

03 / Model development

Pretraining from Scratch and Post-training

From random initialization through post-training

Starting from random initialization, I build language models and develop their capabilities through SFT, distillation, and reinforcement learning. My work covers architectures, tokenization, data mixtures, and distributed training. I also use controlled training data to study how models acquire knowledge and whether they can make discoveries beyond what they have seen during training.

My work · Model and tokenizer implementation, data pipelines, training recipes, and capability evaluation.

~10B parameters · ~1T tokensKnowledge acquisition and discovery

Ziwei Guan
Research Scientist · Meta

I’m a Research Scientist at Meta. My earlier work at Meta focused on sequential modeling for recommendation systems. My recent work focuses on LLMs, with hands-on experience across pretraining, continuous pretraining, and post-training.

I also explore LLM benchmarking and reinforcement learning environments.

I received my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The Ohio State University, advised by Yingbin Liang. My doctoral work studied reinforcement learning, imitation learning, bandits, and online optimization with theoretical guarantees. Before that, I studied at USTC.
